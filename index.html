About Me

I am a senior at Iowa State University studying Marketing and pursuing a Professional Sales Certificate. Through my education and professional experience, I have developed skills in sales, marketing, communication, and customer relationship building.

My internship experience with Comparion Insurance has provided me with hands-on experience working in a professional sales environment. I have had opportunities to communicate with customers, understand their needs, develop relationships, and gain experience within the insurance industry.

Outside of school and work, I enjoy motorcycles, fishing, spending time outdoors, learning about cars, and working on personal projects. These interests give me opportunities to challenge myself, learn new skills, and step outside of my comfort zone.

My long-term goal is to continue developing professionally in sales and marketing while building strong relationships with customers and helping them find solutions that fit their needs.
